# Minimal zine entry

`留白杂志风` and `留白杂志风格` select `ai-image-generation` with the existing `minimal-zine-editorial` preset. Read [the style contract](minimal-zine-editorial.md) for composition, recipes, numeric constraints, deterministic text overlay, thumbnail review and the one-retry limit.

Use for covers, dividers and quiet editorial imagery. Evidence-dense report bodies, operational dashboards, tables, banker appendices and regulatory pages use their own medium rules.

The registered style pack and source metadata remain in the Registry. A named preset is not approval of a style version; upstream examples remain references with their existing rights boundary.
